# Hit the Road Dude Meme: Origin, Lines and AI Video Remakes

A short reference for the **Hit the Road Dude meme**, also known as the **Lock 'Em Up meme** or the **State Property meme**: where the clip comes from, the lines in order, how it spread, and how to make and post your own **Hit the Road Dude AI video** starring your cat or dog.

## One clip, three names

| Name | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Hit the Road Dude | The send-off line, and the name most TikTok captions use |
| Lock 'Em Up | The driver says someone told the owner to have trespassers locked up; Know Your Meme files the clip under this name |
| State Property | The fishermen's excuse for fishing at the pond |

They are not three memes. They are three lines from the same short argument, which is why "hit the road dude lock em up" and "hit the road dude this is private property" all lead to the same clip. The full background is in this [lock em up meme](https://memevideoai.com/lock-em-up-meme) explainer.

## The original video

The Hit the Road Dude original was posted on TikTok on **August 21, 2025** by **@bird28thegr8**. An older man in a white pickup truck pulls up to a pond where two men are fishing. They say they thought the pond was state property. He repeats "State?" in disbelief, sends them off with "hit the road, dude" and tells them it ain't no state property, it is private property. The clip passed **13.7 million views** in about three weeks.

This repo does not host or re-upload the original clip. The Know Your Meme entry listed under Sources documents the first post.

## The lines, in order

1. **The excuse:** the fishermen thought the pond was state property, public land anyone can fish on.
2. **The disbelief:** "State?"
3. **The send-off:** "Hit the road, dude." A thumb over the shoulder.
4. **The correction:** "This ain't no state property, this is private property."
5. **The threat:** "Lock 'em up."

The comedy is in the rhythm: a confident excuse, a baffled "State?", then a flat send-off with no room for debate. That three-beat structure is why the clip works as a sound even when you cannot see the truck.

## How the meme spread

| Date | What happened |
|---|---|
| Aug 21, 2025 | The original fishing-spot video is posted on TikTok |
| Aug 24, 2025 | The first known lip-dub, by @130zay, reaches 2.3 million views in three weeks |
| Early Sep 2025 | A green-screen version of the "lock 'em up" line turns it into an editing template |
| Late Sep 2026 | The first AI remakes appear; one of the earliest puts a dog in the driver's seat |
| Oct 2026 | AI remakes take over, many starring cats and dogs, with the camera, timing and lines unchanged |

Each stage made the meme easier to reuse: the lip-dub turned the audio into a sound anyone could perform, the green-screen template let editors paste the driver into other scenes, and the AI remakes removed the editing entirely.

## AI remakes and the Hit the Road Dude cat

Instead of lip-syncing the lines, creators now use AI video models to put someone else behind the wheel while keeping the truck, the camera and the timing. Cats and dogs are the favourite versions, and the orange tabby "Hit the Road Dude cat" is one of the most common.

## Make your own version

Photos that work best:

- Cats, dogs and other pets, stuffed animals, figurines, or characters you drew yourself
- One subject per photo, face filling at least a third of the frame, eyes and ears visible, even light
- A photo taken at eye level looks most natural behind the steering wheel

Avoid photos of real people (yourself included), characters owned by someone else, group shots, and tiny or blurry subjects.

## Post it on TikTok, Reels and Shorts

1. **Keep the sound on.** TikTok lists it as an original sound, and other people can reuse it, which is how remakes spread. Do not layer the original TikTok's audio on top.
2. **Turn on the AI label.** TikTok has an AI-generated content toggle; YouTube has an altered or synthetic content disclosure.
3. **Add two on-screen captions** for viewers on mute: "STATE?" over the pause and "HIT THE ROAD, DUDE." on the send-off.
4. **Write a setup caption** instead of repeating the quote, for example "My dog when the mail carrier steps on the lawn".
5. **Add a few hashtags:** #hittheroaddude, #lockemup, #stateproperty, plus one for your pet such as #catsoftiktok.
