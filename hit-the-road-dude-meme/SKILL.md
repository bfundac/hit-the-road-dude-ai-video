# Hit the Road Dude meme video

Use this skill when someone wants to make or post their own version of the Hit the Road Dude meme, also searched as the Lock 'Em Up meme or the State Property meme.

## 1. Know the meme

The original is a TikTok posted on August 21, 2025 by @bird28thegr8. An older man in a white pickup truck pulls up to a pond where two men are fishing. Know Your Meme files it as Lock 'Em Up / State Property, and all three names point to the same clip. For the full background, see the [lock em up meme](https://memevideoai.com/lock-em-up-meme) story.

A remake has to keep these beats, in this order:

1. The excuse: the fishermen thought the pond was state property.
2. The disbelief: "State?"
3. The send-off: "Hit the road, dude."
4. The correction: "This ain't no state property, this is private property."
5. The threat: someone told the owner to "lock 'em up".

The joke is the rhythm: a confident excuse, a baffled pause on "State?", then a flat send-off with no room for debate. Never cut the pause.

## 2. Check the photo

Ask for one photo and check it before going further.

- Works: the user, or a friend who agreed to it; a cat, dog, rabbit, hamster or bird; a stuffed animal, figurine or toy; a character the user drew.
- Does not work: a real person who has not agreed; characters or logos owned by someone else (cartoon, anime, film or game characters); group shots; tiny or blurry subjects, heavy filters, sunglasses over the eyes.
- Best results: one subject facing the camera with the whole head in view, the face filling at least a third of the frame, eyes and ears visible, even light, taken at eye level. JPG or PNG, at least 300 pixels on each side.

If the photo fails a check, say which one and ask for another.

## 4. Write the captions

On-screen text carries the joke for people watching on mute. Make it large and centered, away from the bottom of the frame where the app's caption and buttons sit:

- "STATE?" over the pause
- "HIT THE ROAD, DUDE." on the send-off

If the user wants a single caption, use the full line instead: "hit the road dude, this is private property".

Post caption: give the setup, meaning what the pet's "private property" is, instead of repeating the quote. Write three options for the user's subject, in the style of:

- "POV: you sat in the cat's chair"
- "My dog when the mail carrier steps on the lawn"
- "The hamster found out I opened his cage"

Hashtags: five or six at most. Use #hittheroaddude, #lockemup and #stateproperty, plus one for the animal such as #catsoftiktok or #dogsoftiktok.

## 5. Post it

- Keep the video's own sound on. Do not add the original TikTok sound on top: two versions of the exchange ruin the pause.
- Turn on TikTok's AI-generated content label. On YouTube Shorts, tick the altered or synthetic content disclosure. On Reels, use the AI label if the app offers it.
- Upload the file as it is. 9:16 fits all three apps, so there is nothing to crop.
- For a GIF to send in chats, any MP4-to-GIF converter works, but the GIF loses the voice, and most of the joke is in the delivery.

## Do not

- Present the video as real footage of a real event or person. It is a parody.
- Use a photo of someone who has not agreed to be in the video.
- Switch off the AI label to make it look more real.
